BlockML Studio Privacy

Updated 25 September 2026

BlockML Studio is a free coding tool made by CODE AI for school students. It is designed so that students' work, pictures and camera images stay on their own computer. This page lists everything the site does with data, in plain words.

No accounts, no tracking

There are no accounts or logins. The site has no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and doesn't collect names, emails or other personal information.

Your projects

Projects are saved as .sb3 files on your computer when you use File > Save to your computer. The editor may also keep automatic restore points and your settings in this browser's storage on your computer. None of this is uploaded anywhere. Cloud variables work like normal variables and are not shared online.

Camera and AI

The AI blocks (Face, Hand & Pose) use the camera only while a project uses them, and only after the browser asks you for permission. Camera images are processed on your computer by AI models that run inside the page. Camera images are never sent to anyone and never saved. Image models that students train are saved inside their project file as numbers, not as photos.

What the site downloads, and from where

  • This site (hosted by Vercel): the editor itself and the AI model files. Like any website, the hosting service sees standard request details such as your IP address and the time.
  • Scratch's asset server (cdn.assets.scratch.mit.edu): the pictures and sounds in the sprite, costume, backdrop and sound libraries, when you open those libraries. Nothing about you or your project is sent.
  • TurboWarp's server: only the contributor pictures on the Credits page.

Links to other sites (BlockML, TurboWarp, Scratch, GitHub) only open when you click them.

Questions

BlockML Studio is open source, so anyone can check what it does: source code on GitHub. Questions or concerns: open an issue there, or contact CODE AI.
